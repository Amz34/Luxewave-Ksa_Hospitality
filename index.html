Common Questions

Answers Before You Ask.

What is the minimum order quantity?

It depends on the category. Share your room count and the items you need, and we will confirm the MOQ together with your quotation.

Can we start with one room?

Yes. Sample and trial orders are how most properties begin with us — approve the standard first, then scale to full inventory.

Do you deliver outside Riyadh?

We supply 135+ properties across the Kingdom, including Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam and Al Khobar. Share your delivery address and required date for scheduling.

Do products come with compliance documents?

Amenity production is GMPC certified, and we are SFDA-registered, ISO 9001 and SASO certified. Licence and conformity copies are shared on request for your procurement file.

Can amenities carry our own brand?

Yes — private-label programmes cover fragrance selection, bottle and label design, and carton branding, produced on the same certified line.
